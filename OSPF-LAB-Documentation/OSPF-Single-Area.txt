OSPF - Single Area



Router1
Konfigurasi Router 1:

Router1(config)#int fa0/0
Router1(config-if)#ip address 12.12.12.1 255.255.255.0
Router1(config-if)#description TO Router2
Router1(config-if)#no sh
Router1(config-if)#int lo0
Router1(config-if)#ip address 1.1.1.1 255.255.255.255
Router1(config)#router ospf 1
Router1(config-router)#network 12.12.12.0 0.0.0.255 area 0
Router1(config-router)#network 1.1.1.1 0.0.0.0 area 0
